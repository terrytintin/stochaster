How it Works

Create stunning print-ready files in seconds.

1

Upload Your Graphic

Click the "Open Image" button and load your high-resolution artwork directly into the browser. Everything stays local on your machine—no cloud uploads, ensuring maximum privacy and zero latency.

2

Knockout the Background

Select the "Remove BG" tool from the top toolbar to automatically strip away solid white or black outer backgrounds from your subject. Use the "Background" layer at the bottom of the sidebar to visualize the transparency with different colors.

3

Dial in the Perfect Raster

Use the layer controls to experiment with different engines. Try a standard Halftone at 45 degrees, switch to the organic "Worms" Stochastic preset, or overlay a Sine Wave layer. Drag layers to reorder them and change their blend modes to construct complex effects.

4

Export True Transparency

Once you are happy with the visual density, ensure the bottom "Background" visibility eye-icon is toggled off. Hit Export to instantly download a perfectly separated, transparent PNG ready to be dropped into your print-RIP software or directly onto a mockup.
